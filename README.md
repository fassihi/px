# PX

Codename for a short action side-scroller set in a sci-fi world inspired by ancient Persian art.
A Dead Mage project by Amir (design, code direction) and Soheil (character and environment art).

Design documents live in Notion: [PX](https://app.notion.com/p/deadmage/PX-3ecc022a513e80afa668e90b61bf2c1b).
This repository holds the game project and its technical conventions.

## Requirements

- **Unity 6000.3.11f1** (Unity 6.3 LTS), installed through Unity Hub.
- **Git LFS.** Run `git lfs install` once per machine before cloning. Textures, models, audio and other binary files are stored through it.

## Open and play

1. In Unity Hub, add the `PX/` folder as a project and open it with 6000.3.11f1.
2. Open `Assets/PX/Scenes/Level01.unity` (the 20-minute level, "The Gate Halls") or `Assets/PX/Scenes/Graybox.unity`
   (the test level) and press Play.
3. `Assets/PX/Scenes/Locomotion.unity` is where Rana's weight is being worked out: plain daylight, a closer camera and
   only walking, running, crouching and one jump. See below.

## Locomotion scene

A heavier, slower Rana, for the cinematic feel being explored (2026-10-28). Her tuning there is its own:
`Config/PlayerMoveLocomotion.asset` and `Config/HeroineAnimationLocomotion.asset`; the levels keep theirs until the
new feel is settled. She walks (Mixamo's Catwalk Walk Forward Crossed, `Walk.fbx`) and runs with Run held (Left Shift,
left stick press or right trigger), picks up and loses speed slowly, sinks into her knees before a jump, has one jump
and little say over it in the air, lands with a dip, and her body swings round when she turns instead of snapping.
Her idle is Mixamo's Weight Shift Idle (`Idle Weight Shift.fbx`); a standing jump plays the whole of Jumping And
Landing In Place (`Jump Standing.fbx`: its squat is the gather, its air part is stretched to her time in the air) and
lands in Landing From Jump (`Land Heavy.fbx`); a running jump is Female Forward Running Jump (`Jump Running
Stride.fbx`, a striding leap, not a tuck) landing in Landing From Running Jump Into Run Forward (`Land Running
Heavy.fbx`). The clip times are in `LocomotionBuilder.FillHeavyClips`. The camera is closer and trails her. Dash, strikes, guard, interact and flask are off there (`PlayerInputSource`'s
Locomotion Only). The scene is generated by `LocomotionBuilder` (menu PX > Locomotion > Rebuild Scene); its two configs
are only created when missing.

**Camera tuner.** In play mode the Locomotion scene's camera can be set by hand (`CameraTuner`): right drag orbits,
scroll changes the distance, middle drag raises or lowers the point it looks at, Alt + scroll or `[` `]` the field of
view, `,` `.` the look-ahead, `-` `=` how much it trails her; Backspace goes back to the starting view, F1 hides the
panel. The panel shows the camera's height above her feet and how far out from her it sits. **F5 saves** the view to
`Assets/PX/Config/LocomotionCamera.json` (`CameraTuning`), and the next rebuild of the scene uses it.

In the graybox, run to the right to see the same gameplay through several camera angles: side view, a three-quarter
view through the turn, a pulled-back view at the training dummies, a view from behind, then the side again for
Enemy A's arena, a raised view for the climbing walls and the side view for Enemy B at the end.

## Level 01: The Gate Halls

A 20-minute draft of the game, built from Soheil's concept drafts (the plan and the decisions behind it are in
`docs/Level01-Plan.md`). One rail of about 590 m through two acts:

| Rail | Zone | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–60 | The Threshold | She wakes at a shrine. A gap over the water, a step, a ledge; a guard with its back to her (the first takedown); an alcove high on the wall for later. |
| 79–140 | The Flooded Court | Broken platforms over dark water, two sentinels drifting above, a ledge over the middle with a cache of light for whoever can tether up to it. |
| 140–230 | The Guardhouse | Low walls to crouch behind, four guards on their rounds, a sealed door and the lever beside the last guard. The gallery runs overhead. |
| 230–300 | The Gate | The mandala gate. The Tether on its plinth, a knight guarding it, lamps up to the gallery (which leads back over the guardhouse) and on across the chasm. |
| 317–430 | The Scaffolds | Act 2. Scaffolds climbing through the bazaar's underside: platforms, a wall to catch, lamps, sentinels, a knight on a walkway. |
| 430–520 | The Bazaar | Hanging walkways under neon signs, a two-guard patrol among cover, a sentinel, the last shrine. |
| 538–592 | The Face Shrine | The Captain before the giant face. Gates fall when she enters; its fall ends the draft. |

**Shrines** light as she passes and become where she gets up. Resting at one (Interact) refills health, stamina
and flasks and brings the regular enemies back. **Light** is what enemies leave; on death she drops it where she
fell and can take it back once; at a shrine it buys a flask slot or more health. **Stealth:** guards see only in
front of them, crouching behind cover hides her, blows make noise, and an unaware guard taken from behind dies at
once; marks over their heads show what they know. **Stagger:** enough blows (or a parry) throw an enemy open for an
execution. **The Tether**, found in the gate chamber, pulls her up to glowing lamps: the upper routes and the way into
Act 2 need it. **Sentinels** fire slow bolts she can dodge, block or parry back. **The camera** cuts in for finishers,
the last kill of a fight and the boss's fall, and plays a wide reveal with the place's name on first entering a zone.

## How the game is put together

**The rail.** She moves along a `Rail`: a spline that can curve freely through the 3D world.
Gameplay code only knows two numbers, distance along the rail and height. `CharacterMotor`
turns those into 3D movement with collisions.

**The camera.** `RailCamera` describes its view relative to the rail's direction, so a side view stays
a side view around corners. A `CameraZone` gives any stretch of rail its own angle. Changing the
camera never touches gameplay code.

**Tuning is data.** Movement numbers live in `Config/PlayerMove.asset` (including the dodge and ledges), the
combo's timings in `Config/PlayerLightCombo.asset`, stamina, guard, parry, counter and death in
`Config/PlayerCombat.asset`, and each enemy's in `Config/EnemyA.asset` and `Config/EnemyB.asset`. Change them in
the Inspector, during play if you like.

**The fight is soulslike.** Strikes, dashes, dodges and blocks cost stamina, which comes back after a pause; an
empty bar means no attack. The dodge back has invulnerable frames. Guard blocks frontal blows for stamina and a
little chip damage, and breaks when stamina runs out; pressed just before a blow lands it parries, throwing the
enemy off balance and opening a counter. Enemies have poise: light strikes do not interrupt their swings. When she
falls she gets up at the last checkpoint and the enemies still standing return to their posts.

**The scenes are generated.** `Graybox.unity` is written by `Scripts/Editor/GrayboxBuilder.cs` and
`Level01.unity` by `Scripts/Editor/LevelBuilder.cs`, each overwritten on every rebuild (menus: PX > Graybox > Rebuild
Scene, PX > Level 01 > Rebuild Scene). To change a level, change its builder. Config assets, materials and prefabs are
only created when missing, so edits made to them in the editor are kept; `CharacterBuilder.UpgradeConfigs` fills fields
added since a config was made. Hand-made scenes go in their own files.

## Command line

Close the project in the Unity editor first. Unity allows one process per project.

```bash
UNITY="/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.3.11f1/Unity.app/Contents/MacOS/Unity"
```

Rebuild the graybox scene, or Level 01:

```bash
"$UNITY" -batchmode -quit -projectPath PX -executeMethod PX.EditorTools.GrayboxBuilder.Build -logFile -
"$UNITY" -batchmode -quit -projectPath PX -executeMethod PX.EditorTools.LevelBuilder.Build -logFile -
```

Type-check the code without the editor (Unity's own compiler, no import, no tests):

```bash
Tools/typecheck.sh all
```

Run the tests (leave out `-quit`, the test runner exits by itself):

```bash
"$UNITY" -batchmode -projectPath PX -runTests -testPlatform EditMode -testResults TestResults-EditMode.xml -logFile -
"$UNITY" -batchmode -projectPath PX -runTests -testPlatform PlayMode -testResults TestResults-PlayMode.xml -logFile -
```

Render screenshots of the camera views, close-ups of her moves and the fights while running the play mode tests:

```bash
PX_SCREENSHOT_DIR="$PWD/Screenshots" "$UNITY" -batchmode -projectPath PX -runTests -testPlatform PlayMode -testResults TestResults-PlayMode.xml -logFile -
```

In the editor, the same tests are under Window > General > Test Runner.
